People & culture

Ambition without
the unnecessary pressure.

We want to create a place where people can do thoughtful work, learn from each other and enjoy being part of what they are building.

How we work

A good environment
is not an extra.

We believe a chill, supportive working environment and ambitious goals can coexist. People should be able to ask questions, share unfinished ideas and disagree respectfully without feeling that every interaction is a competition.

We value clear communication, trust, sustainable progress and the freedom to take ownership. Roles help organise work, but they should never stop good ideas from being heard.

A simple belief

“People should feel that they are part of building something, not just completing tasks for it.”
